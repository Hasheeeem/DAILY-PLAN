Targeted Job Search Protocol

  • Role Focus: Frontend / Full-Stack / AI Integration roles that your actual project outcomes support. Keep tailored resume variations.
  • Weekly Cadence: 4 to 6 strong, tailored applications + 2 to 3 direct networking/recruiter messages per week. Quality over mindless volume.
  • Dedicated Time Blocks: Monday 45m (Applications), Wednesday 45m (Interview practice: 20m technical coding, 5m speak aloud, 20m behavioral), Wednesday 75m (Portfolio evidence), Saturday 45m (Career block).
  • Application Tracker Fields: Company, Role, Country, Job Link, Fit Reason, Resume Version, Application Date, Status, Next Action.
  • Follow-Up Boundary: Exactly one polite follow-up 7 to 10 business days after applying if a contact route exists. Never send daily pestering messages.
  • Truthful Proof: Describe what you built, what changed, and how it was tested. Never invent metrics, team sizes, or technologies.

Flexibility, Dining Out & Weighing Rules

  • No Food Banned: Traditional staples (puttu, idlis, dosas, appam, rice, chapatis) all fit seamlessly by adjusting starch and oil portions.
  • Bakery Snacks: Banana chips and savory snacks can quietly displace planned nutrition. Choose them deliberately in modest portions rather than bingeing guiltily.
  • Dining Out: Outside meals can be planned: estimate portions, add curd or salad, skip sugary drinks, and resume normal eating at the next meal. Never starve before or after a social meal.
  • Weigh-In Rule: Weigh 3 mornings a week after toilet and before breakfast. Compare weekly averages. If flat for 3 weeks, adjust cooking oil or add 1,000 steps.

Physical Training, Strength Progression & Walking Targets

Starting with progressive bodyweight strength and walking bases. Strength days on Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday; walking on Tuesday, Thursday, and Sunday; Friday recovery.

Equipment Boundary: Your only stated equipment is one 1 kg dumbbell. This assists light shoulder work (arm circles, W-raises) but cannot meaningfully load the legs. Rely on progressive bodyweight. Use stable chairs or kitchen counters for support. Never anchor resistance to doors, weak tables, or improvised hanging fixtures.

8-Week Progressive Overload Strength Ladder

Stage Frequency Sets & Reps Progression Criteria & Form Rules
Week 1 (Establish) 2 days (Wed & Sat only) 1 easy set of 4–6 moves Stop with 2–3 reps in reserve. Focus on warm-up and form. Overrides full schedules.
Week 2 (Adapt) 2 days (Wed & Sat) 2 sets per movement Progress to 2 sets only if fully recovered from Week 1 without joint pain.
Weeks 3 & 4 (Consolidate) 3 days (Mon, Wed, Sat) 2 sets per movement Full 3-day strength cadence. Exhale through effort; rest 60–90 seconds between sets.
Weeks 5 Onward (Overload) 3 days (Mon, Wed, Sat) Add reps, then 3rd set Once top reps feel easy for 2 consecutive sessions: lower counter push-ups, deeper squats, or +5s plank. Never increase everything at once.

Legs & Posterior Chain

  • Chair Squats: 8–12 reps. Tap chair lightly without collapsing. Feet flat, knees tracking over toes. Progress to freestanding deep squats.
  • Supported Split Squats: 6–8 reps/side (Wednesday). Hold stable counter if needed for balance.
  • Glute Bridges: 10–15 reps. Drive through heels, 1-second squeeze at top.
  • Hip Hinges: 10–12 reps. Unloaded hinge teaches safe spinal alignment.

Upper Body & Core

  • Wall / Counter Push-Ups: 6–10 reps. Rigid plank line. Progress by lowering hands from wall to kitchen counter.
  • Prone W Raises: 8–12 reps. Lie face down, draw elbows to ribs, squeeze shoulder blades. Counteracts screen slouching.
  • Plank: 15–25 seconds. Abdominal brace, no sagging lower back.
  • Side Plank from Knees: 10–20 seconds/side. Builds lateral hip and spine stamina.

Walking & Running

  • Week 1 Walking: Add 10–15 min daily in 5-minute pieces (~1,500–2,500 steps).
  • Weeks 2–4: Add 500 steps/day; build toward 3,000–4,500 steps.
  • Weeks 5–8: 5,000–7,000 steps. (7,000–9,000 optional).
  • Break Sitting: Walk for 2–5 minutes every 45–60 minutes.
  • Running Base: Wait until 30m brisk walking is easy for 3–4 weeks. 2x weekly: 6 rounds of (30s jog + 90s walk).

Skincare, Hair Styling, Treatment Boundaries & Migraines

Evidence-based essentials. Strict clinical safety boundaries for hair care, sun protection, and migraine management.

Critical Hair Treatment Boundaries & Safety Protocol

  • Minoxidil Regimen: Standard 1 ml twice daily is a reference example, NOT confirmation of your pump volume. Use ONLY your current dermatologist-confirmed label instructions. Apply to a completely dry scalp before styling.
  • Drying Protocol: 4 hours before washing; 2 to 4 hours drying before resting on a pillow (the 5:50 PM slot leaves 5h 10m before bed). Never use a hot hairdryer over wet medication.
  • NO DERMAROLLER: Do NOT use home microneedling. The FDA has not cleared microneedling with drugs. Needling changes absorption depth unpredictably and risks infection, severe irritation, and scarring.
  • NO HOMEMADE ROSEMARY WATER: Rosemary oil was tested against 2% minoxidil in a small study; homemade rosemary water does NOT equate to 5% minoxidil. Never put homemade solutions on needle-punctured skin.
  • Dermatologist Follow-Up: Book a review for early-onset thinning. Bring previous prescription, current spray bottle, and past procedure records.
  • Stop Immediately & Seek Medical Help: If you experience palpitations, chest pain, dizziness, fainting, or unexplained swelling.

Hair Styling for Thin Wavy Hair

  • Best Styling Choice: Lightweight flexible-hold mousse for waves/curls. Walnut-sized dollop on barely damp lengths (avoid scalp). Scrunch once, air dry untouched, then gently scrunch out the crispness.
  • Alternative: Light matte styling cream (half a pea to one pea size) if mousse feels wrong.
  • Skip Completely: Heavy waxes, oily pomades, stiff gels (weighs hair down, exposes scalp), and drying sea-salt sprays.
  • Hair Washing Cadence: 2 to 3 times weekly (Monday, Thursday, Saturday). Shampoo scalp gently, condition hair lengths, detangle with wide-tooth comb. No rough towel drying.

Skincare Essentials & Health Rules

  • 3 Essentials First: Gentle non-stripping cleanser (morning rinse if clean), lightweight moisturizer (glycerin/ceramides), and broad-spectrum SPF 50 sunscreen.
  • Sunscreen Protection: Apply ~two finger lengths to face, ears, neck, and exposed scalp 15 min before sun. Reapply every 2 hours outdoors.
  • Hydration: 2.3 to 2.7 liters of drinks daily (mostly water). Pale yellow urine benchmark. 400–800 ml/hr in heavy sweating.
  • Migraine Management: Consistent sleep, meals, and hydration. Hard 1:00 PM caffeine cutoff. Reduce screen glare; keep a simple headache diary. Lower stimulation during attacks.
  • Supplements: Vitamin D weekly/monthly prescription MUST NOT be taken daily. Omega-3: check EPA/DHA; fish intake can replace supplements. Multivitamin: do not duplicate nutrients.

High-Value Work & Ownership

  • 3 Weekly Outcomes: Choose exactly 3 at the week's start: (1) One client delivery outcome that builds trust, (2) One revenue-driving sales result, (3) One operational improvement that removes repeated manual work.
  • 1 Daily Finishable Deliverable: Break vague goals like “build backend” into concrete testable endpoints or client decisions.
  • Explicit Ownership: Lead covers CEO, sales, dev, delivery. Assign one explicit owner, expected outcome, and due point for every task. Keep meetings strictly for debates/decisions.
  • Direct Sales (30m): Mon–Thu qualified prospect follow-ups, proposals, and decisions. Track cash received and contracts, not just message counts.

AI Waiting Protocol & Client Windows

  • Define Expected Output First: Always write down the expected code output and test criteria before prompting AI.
  • Waiting Task List: Keep an active waiting list (review git diffs, inspect docs, write test cases, prepare next prompt). NO jumping to Instagram or WhatsApp!
  • Deliberate Shutdown: Record completed items, exact tomorrow step 1, blocker owner. Check emergency channel briefly. Close work. Unfinished work reschedules to next block.
  • International Client Calls: Offer 2:00–3:15 PM IST first; 7:35–8:15 PM IST exception max 1x/week. Prefer async recorded Loom demos. Never past midnight.
